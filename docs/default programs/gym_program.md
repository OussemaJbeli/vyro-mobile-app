# Gym Training Program

**Cycle:** 3 Training Days → 1 Rest Day → Repeat
**Goal:** Muscle growth + strength + fat loss support
**Intensity:** Keep ~1–3 reps in reserve on most sets

# 🔄 Repeat

**Day 1 → Day 2 → Day 3 → Rest → Day 1 → Day 2 → Day 3 → Rest → ...**

## Training Rules

* **Warm-up:** 5–10 min + lighter warm-up sets before heavy exercises.
* **Rest between sets:** 2–3 min for compound exercises; 60–120 sec for isolation exercises.
* **Progressive overload:** When you reach the top of the rep range with good form, increase the weight slightly.
* **Do not train every set to failure.** Usually stop with ~1–3 good reps remaining.
* Prioritize **controlled technique and full comfortable range of motion**.
* Keep the program consistent instead of constantly changing exercises.
