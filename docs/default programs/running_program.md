# Running Program

**Goal:** Fat loss + endurance + speed + leg strength  
**Level:** Normal/fit user  
**Weekly structure:** 5 running days + 2 recovery days

## Weekly Plan

| Day | Workout | Duration |
|---|---|---:|
| **Monday** | Incline Endurance | 30 min |
| **Tuesday** | Speed Intervals | 25 min |
| **Wednesday** | Incline Power | 30 min |
| **Thursday** | Rest / Recovery | — |
| **Friday** | Speed Endurance | 30 min |
| **Saturday** | Long Aerobic Run | 40–45 min |
| **Sunday** | Full Rest | — |

### Monday — Incline Endurance
- **5 min:** 4–4.5 mph, 1% incline
- **20 min:** 6 mph, 3% incline
- **5 min:** 4–4.5 mph, 1% incline
- **Focus:** Aerobic endurance + steady calorie burn.

### Tuesday — Speed Intervals
- **5 min warm-up:** 4–5 mph, 1%
- **16 min:** 8 rounds of **1 min at 9 mph + 1 min at 6 mph**, 1%
- **4 min cooldown:** easy pace
- **Focus:** Speed + high-intensity cardio.

### Wednesday — Incline Power
- **5 min warm-up:** 4–5 mph, 1%
- **5 rounds:** 3 min at **5–5.5 mph, 6–8% incline** + 2 min at 4 mph, 1%
- **5 min cooldown**
- **Focus:** Leg strength + conditioning.

### Thursday — Recovery
**No hard running.**  
Walking, mobility, or light stretching is fine.

### Friday — Speed Endurance
- **7 min warm-up:** gradually 4 → 6 mph
- **4 rounds:** 4 min at **7–7.5 mph** + 2 min at 4.5 mph
- **3 min cooldown**
- **Focus:** Holding a fast pace for longer.

### Saturday — Long Aerobic
- **5 min:** 4–4.5 mph, 1%
- **30–35 min:** 5.5–6 mph, 2–3%
- **5 min:** easy cooldown
- **Total:** 40–45 min
- **Focus:** Aerobic base + calorie expenditure.

### Sunday — Full Rest
Complete rest or normal daily activity.

## Progression

Keep the same structure for **3–4 weeks**. Progress gradually:
- Add **2–5 min** to the Saturday run.
- Increase speed slightly only when the current pace feels comfortable.
- Increase **one variable at a time** (speed, incline, or duration).
- Every ~4–5 weeks, reduce volume by **20–25%** for a recovery week.

## Important Rules

- Hard days should be **hard but controlled**, not all-out.
- Do not turn every run into a maximum-effort workout.
- If your legs are heavily fatigued from gym training, reduce treadmill intensity.
- Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath.
- For fat loss, training works best alongside a sustainable calorie deficit and adequate protein.
