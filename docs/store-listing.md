# Store listing draft (Google Play)

**App name:** VYRO

**Short description (≤ 80):** Plan, train and track gym, running and abs — private and offline.

**Full description:**

VYRO is your training plan and log in one place: gym sessions, treadmill intervals and
six-pack work, planned for every day of your week and tracked set by set.

• Your week, planned: a routine that fills itself every week, with times you can change any
  day. See each day's score — Excellent, Good or Bad — and a monthly status.
• Gym and abs: sets, weights, reps and rest timers, exercise swaps and pictures.
• Running: an interval runner built for the treadmill — big timer, speed and incline, skip,
  repeat and voice cues, in mph or km/h.
• Progress: weight and measurements, trends, weekly stats, history, and private progress
  photos you can compare.
• Private by design: all data stays on your device. No account. No tracking. Works in
  airplane mode. Optional app lock and local backups you control.

**Privacy statement:** All data stays on your device. No account. No tracking.

**Screenshots (1080×2400, dark unless noted):** Home · Plan (week, with day score) · Plan

**Category:** Health & Fitness · **Content rating:** Everyone · **Data safety:** no data
