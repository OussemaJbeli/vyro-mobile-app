Day 1 — PUSH Chest · Shoulders · Triceps Upper-chest priority, balanced chest development, and enough direct shoulder and triceps work without unnecessary duplication.

1\. Incline Chest Press Upper chest · Incline press machine 3 × 8–12 · 20 kg · Rest 150 s 8–12 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 12 reps → add weight.

2\. Lever Lying Chest Press Middle chest · Horizontal chest press machine 3 × 6–10 · 35 kg · Rest 150 s 6–10 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 10 reps → add weight.

3\. Decline Chest Press Lower chest · Decline press machine 2 × 8–12 · 40 kg · Rest 150 s 8–12 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 12 reps → add weight. Keep this at moderate volume because upper-chest development is your priority.

4\. Pec Deck Chest · Pec Deck machine 3 × 10–15 · 30 kg · Rest 90 s 10–15 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 15 reps → add weight. Use a comfortable stretch without forcing your shoulders backward. This replaces the Multi-Flight chest-fly variation.

5\. Dumbbell Shoulder Press Front and side delts · Seated dumbbell press 2 × 6–10 · 10 kg per dumbbell · Rest 150 s 6–10 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 10 reps → add weight. Keep your back supported and avoid excessive arching.

6\. Dumbbell Lateral Raise Side delts · Dumbbells 3 × 10–15 · 10 kg per dumbbell · Rest 90 s 10–15 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 15 reps → add weight. Raise with control, without swinging or shrugging.

7\. Triceps Pushdown Triceps · Cable machine 3 × 8–12 · 40 kg · Rest 90 s 8–12 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 12 reps → add weight. Keep your elbows close to your sides.

8\. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension Triceps long head · Cable machine 2 × 10–15 · 25 kg · Rest 90 s 10–15 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 15 reps → add weight. Use a comfortable shoulder position and control the stretch.

Push day: 21 working sets. If recovery becomes difficult, reduce the decline press to 1 set before adding or changing exercises.

Day 2 — PULL Lats · Upper back · Rear delts · Biceps · Forearms The goal is to develop both back width and thickness, train the rear delts directly, and combine a strict biceps curl with a neutral-grip curl.

1\. Lat Pulldown Lats · Lat pulldown machine 3 × 8–12 · 45 kg · Rest 150 s 8–12 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 12 reps → add weight. Pull toward your upper chest without swinging or leaning excessively backward.

2\. Seated Cable Row Mid-back · Seated cable row machine 3 × 8–12 · 40 kg · Rest 150 s 8–12 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 12 reps → add weight. Reach forward under control, then row without rocking your torso.

3\. Cable One Arm Twisting Seated Row Lats · Cable machine 2 × 10–15 per side · Start light · Rest 60–90 s 10–15 reps per side · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 15 reps → add weight. Keep your shoulder controlled and bring your upper arm toward your torso. This adds lat isolation; it does not replace the pulldown.

4\. Lever Seated Row Rear delts · Reverse fly machine 3 × 12–15 · Start with a controlled load · Rest 90 s 12–15 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 15 reps → add weight. Lead with your elbows and avoid shrugging. This is the reverse-fly setting, not the chest-fly setting.

5\. Lever Preacher Curl Biceps · Preacher curl machine 3 × 8–12 · 15 kg · Rest 90 s 8–12 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 12 reps → add weight. Keep your upper arms against the pad and avoid bouncing out of the bottom position.

6\. Dumbbell Hammer Curl Brachialis · Brachioradialis · Dumbbells 2 × 10–15 · Select a strict working load · Rest 90 s 10–15 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 15 reps → add weight. Keep your palms facing each other and wrists neutral.

Pull day: 16 working sets. The preacher curl is your primary biceps exercise, while hammer curls provide complementary arm and forearm development. You do not need to add EZ-bar curls on top.

Day 3 — LEGS Quads · Hamstrings · Glutes · Calves · Lower back The plan combines your established hack squat and leg press with the Smith deadlift for hamstrings and glutes. Keep the deadlift technically controlled, especially while running regularly.

1\. Hack Squat Quads · Hack squat machine 3 × 6–10 · 80 kg · Rest 180 s 6–10 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 10 reps → add weight. Keep your hips and back against the pads and use a controlled, comfortable depth.

2\. Smith Deadlift Hamstrings · Glutes · Smith machine 3 × 8–10 · Start with a light, technically manageable load · Rest 180 s 8–10 reps · 2 RIR · all sets at 10 reps → add a small amount of weight. Push your hips backward, maintain a neutral spine, and stop the descent when your hamstrings reach their comfortable limit. Do not chase the floor.

3\. Leg Press Quads · Leg press machine 2 × 8–12 · 100 kg · Rest 150 s 8–12 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 12 reps → add weight. Keep your hips against the pad and avoid locking your knees forcefully.

4\. Leg Curl Hamstrings · Leg curl machine 3 × 10–15 · 40 kg · Rest 90 s 10–15 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 15 reps → add weight. Control the return phase. If your machine is a seated or lying version, use the same progression rule.

5\. Machine Calf Raise Calves · Calf raise machine 3 × 10–15 · 90 kg · Rest 90 s 10–15 reps · 1–2 RIR · all sets at 15 reps → add weight. Use a full, comfortable stretch and pause briefly at the top.

6\. Back Extension Lower back · 45° back-extension bench 1–2 × 12–15 · Bodyweight · Rest 90 s 12–15 reps · 2 RIR · once all sets reach 15 reps with excellent control, consider a small additional load. Finish with your torso aligned with your legs; don't hyperextend your lower back. Start with 1 set because the Smith deadlift already trains your posterior chain.
